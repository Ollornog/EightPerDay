- Bookings per day on projects or clients with an order number and hours; the list shows what is not booked on
  projects (day minus the sum of its projects), per day and week.
- A printable weekly report: carry-over, difference, balance, and per day the clients with numbers and hours.
- All employers in one list, a column each only on the days they are active; per employer its pay (hour, day or half
  day, job with a client and an amount, extras such as commission, photos and tips, taken in cash or by card) and the
  earnings per day, week and the average day. Optionally a place of work with kilometres, and standby.
- An optional estimate of pay from a salary or an hourly rate, with 13th and 14th month pay.
