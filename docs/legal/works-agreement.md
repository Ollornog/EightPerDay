# Works agreement on time recording and leave planning (skeleton)

> **No legal advice, not checked by a lawyer** (*keine Rechtsberatung, nicht juristisch geprüft*). A skeleton to
> adapt, not a finished agreement. In Germany the introduction and use of a technical system that is able to monitor
> behaviour or performance is subject to co-determination (§ 87 Abs. 1 Nr. 6 BetrVG; the objective ability counts,
> not the intention); start and end of the working time and their distribution (Nr. 2) and the principles of leave
> and the leave plan (Nr. 5) as well. In Austria §§ 96, 96a ArbVG apply instead. Public service: staff representation
> law, not covered here. A works agreement does not make lawful what data protection law forbids (CJEU C-65/23).
> Square brackets mark what the parties fill in.

**Between** [employer] **and** the works council of [establishment]

## § 1 Subject and scope

1. This agreement governs the recording of working time and the planning of leave with the software EightPerDay
   ("the system") in [establishment / departments].
2. It applies to all employees within the meaning of § 5 BetrVG [except: …].

## § 2 Purposes

1. The system is used only to
   - record working time as required by law (§ 3 Abs. 2 Nr. 1 ArbSchG as interpreted by BAG 1 ABR 22/21, § 16 Abs. 2
     ArbZG, § 17 MiLoG where it applies),
   - keep time and leave accounts and prepare payroll,
   - plan and approve leave [and approve working time, if the approval feature is switched on].
2. Any other use, in particular to assess the behaviour or performance of employees, is not allowed. Data from the
   system may not be used for measures under labour law [except: …, with the involvement of the works council].

## § 3 Data

1. The system stores the data listed in the data inventory of the version in use (`docs/data-inventory.json`):
   working times (start, end, breaks or durations), absences and their type, leave, corrections with their history,
   and the access log. Sickness is recorded as a flag without a diagnosis or a note.
2. Private records that employees keep in the system for themselves are not part of this agreement and are not
   accessible to the employer.

## § 4 Functions switched on

| Function | On / off | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Approval of periods and leave | [on/off] | Who decides: [role]. A rejection always gives a reason. |
| Supervisor's present/absent view | [on/off] | Only the own team, only "present" or "absent", no history, no reasons. |
| Night, Sunday and holiday surcharges | [on/off] | Rules as in [collective agreement / contract]. |

Switching a function on or off needs the prior consent of the works council. The system records who switched it on
when, with the reference to this agreement.

## § 5 Access and roles

1. Employees see all their own data, including the history of corrections and who accessed their data.
2. [Supervisors] see the data of their team that was handed in for approval, [HR] the data needed for payroll.
   Administrators of the system have technical access only [details].
3. Every read and change of an employee's data by someone else is logged and visible to the employee.

## § 6 Corrections

Corrections never overwrite: the original value stays with who, when and why. If someone other than the employee
corrects a record, the employee is informed [and confirms or objects within … days].

## § 7 Evaluations

Only the following evaluations are allowed: [time and leave accounts, overtime, rest-period hints to the employee, …].
Free queries across persons are [not allowed / allowed only for HR, logged].

## § 8 Retention and deletion

| Data | Retention |
|---|---|
| Time records and their history | [3] years after the end of the year (at least 2: § 16 Abs. 2 ArbZG, § 17 MiLoG) |
| Notices about lapsing leave | [3] years after the end of the year |
| Access log | [3] years after the end of the year |

Deletion is automatic. During a dispute the deletion of the data concerned is suspended (legal hold) and resumes when
it ends. Retention under tax and commercial law is checked by [employer].

## § 9 Information and rights of employees

Employees are informed before the system is introduced (Art. 13 GDPR) and can see the information at any time in the
system. They can get a copy of all their data (Art. 15 GDPR) as an export.

## § 10 Changes of the system

Updates that add functions processing personal data, or change existing ones in a way relevant to this agreement, are
presented to the works council beforehand [… weeks].

Effective from [date]; may be terminated with [three months'] notice; [continued effect / no continued effect].
